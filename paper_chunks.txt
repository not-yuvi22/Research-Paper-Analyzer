IEEE GEOSCIENCE AND REMOTE SENSING LETTERS, VOL. 21, 2024 4003605 A Lightweight SAR Ship Detector Using End-to-End Image Preprocessing Network and Channel Feature Guided Spatial Pyramid Pooling Chuxuan Chen , Student Member, IEEE, Yimin Zhang , Ronglin Hu, and Yongtao Yu , Senior Member, IEEE AbstractRecently, in the field of synthetic aperture radar (SAR) ship detection, deep-learning-based methods have made significant strides in terms of detection accuracy and speed. However, small-scale targets and complex backgrounds remain a formidable obstacle to SAR ship detection. To overcome the aforementioned challenges, this letter proposes LiteSAR-Net, a lightweight SAR ship detector, to enhance ship detection capabilities in SAR imagery. In detail, an end-to-end image preprocessing network (E2IPNet) is proposed to strengthen context information and expand the network’s effective receptive field. In addition, to prevent the dilution of semantic information, the channel feature guided spatial pyramid pooling (CFGSPP) is proposed, which can adjust the parameters adaptively based on interchannel information.

CHUNK 009 | SECTION: CONCLUSION
============================================================

This letter proposes LiteSAR-Net, an innovative lightweight SAR ship detector, with the intent of enhancing ship detection capabilities in SAR imagery. On the one hand, E2IPNet is designed to expand the network’s effective receptive field and to improve the context information of ships in SAR images. CFGSPP, on the other hand, is proposed to reduce the loss of semantic information and to fuse multiscale ship features.
